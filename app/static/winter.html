Winter savings

What if you moved some usage to cheaper slots?

A what-if on your own readings. For each day, take this share of your energy out of that day's dearest half-hours and put it into the cheapest ones, then re-price it. Nothing is moved above your own busiest half-hour that day, so it never needs a bigger heater or charger than you already ran. Standing charge isn't affected, so it's left out.

When the cheap and dear slots are

How many of each day's 8 cheapest and 8 dearest half-hours fall in each hour, averaged over the winter.
